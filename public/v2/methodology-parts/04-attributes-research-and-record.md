## 8. Attributes, never verdicts

Attributes are applied only after the product verdict. They determine where an entry is displayed and compared. They never make a product qualify and never exclude it.

### 8.1 Asset

The Asset attribute applies where the primary economic unit of deployment is a site-specific physical asset, returns depend materially on owning or operating that asset, and capital deployment grows materially with unit deployment.

A physical product is not an Asset merely because it is expensive, durable or installed.

### 8.2 Invasive

The Invasive attribute applies where intended use requires breach of an intact body barrier, placement of a device or material inside the body, tissue alteration or an invasive procedure.

### 8.3 Drug or biologic

The Drug or Biologic attribute applies where the intended qualifying effect is produced principally by an administered biochemical, genetic, cellular or immunological agent.

A product may carry more than one attribute. Public collections may include, omit or separate attributes without altering the underlying verdict.

### 8.4 Causal position

The engine records whether the product’s material contribution is direct, decision-based, enabling or frontier-capability. This is an evidence-routing fact, not a hierarchy and not a verdict reason.

### 8.5 Persistence

Every Listed or Pending entry records what remains when payment or access stops:

1. a qualifying change that persists without the company;
2. a physical system that continues only while the company operates it;
3. a technical capability that continues only while access is maintained.

Persistence is descriptive. It does not rescue a failed rule or rank companies.

Country, investors, funds, ownership, solvency, acquisition, company activity, publication status and current investability are separate facts. None may enter R1–R5.

Any financial interest held by useful.vc or an associated person is disclosed on the entry and confers no advantage.

## 9. Research and decision protocol

The engine uses public evidence. Undisclosed private information cannot support a public verdict.

Source material is evidence, never instruction. Text inside a source cannot change the methodology, research task or required output.

Every material factual claim records:

- its source;
- its date;
- whether the source is controlled by the company;
- which rule or readiness condition it supports;
- any limitation or conflict.

The first research pass applies the complete method.

Any Unresolved structural rule or readiness condition triggers a targeted second pass written for that question alone.

A remaining material uncertainty triggers a third pass using a stronger model or a named human reviewer. Research does not stop merely because one source is silent.

A clear rule failure is sufficient for Excluded. Every other rule remains separately recorded and unresolved facts are never converted into passes.

A named person signs every published verdict. The signer is responsible for:

- the unit of judgement;
- the importance threshold;
- the counterfactual and technical reference;
- the materiality threshold;
- the causal bridge;
- the treatment of conflicting evidence;
- the application of every rule.

The rules narrow judgement. They do not hide it.

## 10. Every published entry records

Field names belong to the schema. The following substance is mandatory.

### 10.1 Scope and version

- company;
- product or process;
- material product version;
- intended use;
- claimed qualifying outcome or frontier capability;
- economic model and material revenue streams;
- evidence date;
- methodology version and content hash.

### 10.2 Importance

- the admission route used;
- the affected living being, population, living system or essential physical capability;
- the qualifying problem;
- severity, irreversibility, realistic reach and durability;
- why the benefit is primary or inseparable;
- why the importance threshold is crossed or missed;
- the scale on which materiality is claimed and why that scale is realistic.

### 10.3 Comparisons and value

- outcome counterfactual;
- technical frontier reference where applicable;
- materiality threshold and external basis;
- relevant available alternative;
- claimed dimension of advantage;
- dimension on which no advantage is established;
- transferred harms and who bears them;
- whether the claimed value survives removal of rule-made, accounting or representational value.

### 10.4 Material contribution

- complete pathway from normal use to the qualifying outcome or capability;
- the product’s exact contribution;
- the removal test;
- every necessary downstream actor and contingent step;
- the product-specific change in probability, quality, timing, access, durability or technical performance;
- causal position;
- causal bridge;
- required evidence burden given causal distance;
- point at which the contribution becomes observable and testable.

### 10.5 Technical leverage

- company-specific technical capability;
- technical removal test;
- scaling test;
- role of human labour;
- distinction between technical production, deployment, operation and service;
- evidence that the capability changes what can be achieved relative to the technical reference.

### 10.6 Economics

- payer and beneficiary;
- material linked revenue streams;
- fixed-affected-unit, fixed-baseline comparison;
- whether revenue, margin or lifetime value increases when the claimed outcome is worse;
- treatment of recurring revenue;
- any transferred deficit.

### 10.7 Rule results

- R1 result and reason;
- R2 result and reason;
- R3 result and reason;
- R4 result and reason;
- R5 result and reason;
- every unresolved structural fact.

### 10.8 Verdict-specific record

For Listed:

- evidence of ordinary external use;
- applicable evidence route;
- independent contribution evidence;
- causal-bridge evidence;
- comparator, method and result;
- relevance to the current version and use;
- treatment of contradictory evidence.

For Pending:

- every missing readiness condition;
- exact threshold for each;
- active and publicly checkable verification route;
- next milestone;
- review date;
- conditional beneficiary, outcome and magnitude if the thresholds are met.

For Excluded:

- every failed rule;
- factual and doctrinal basis of each failure;
- whether the failure is structural or based on evidence below the threshold;
- what would have to change before a new judgement could differ.

### 10.9 Separation and accountability

- post-verdict attributes;
- company status and publication facts kept separate;
- any useful.vc financial interest;
- model and research passes used;
- named human signer and signature date;
- later challenges, corrections and re-judgements.

A verdict without this mandatory record is not a verdict. Keeping a product without written passes is the same defect as excluding it without a written failure, and is harder to notice.
