# useful.vc — Engine Methodology V2

**Canonical methodology — version 2.0 — 20 August 2026**

This text replaces every earlier selectivity formulation.

The public rules page is the reference text. The classifier prompt and the coverage automation prompt must contain this text identically. All deployed copies must share the same content hash. A mismatch blocks every new verdict write and every publication change until the copies are corrected.

This document contains no examples, named cases, sector lists or expected verdict counts, and none may be added to a deployed copy. Every rule must generate its own instances. A category, label or analogy may help research, but it may never substitute for applying the rule to the product being judged.

Nothing here is negotiable case by case. A factual error may be corrected. A defect in the doctrine may be corrected only by publishing a new version and re-judging every affected entry under the same change.

A specific provision controls over a general one. Every applicable rule is evaluated. Where more than one rule fails, every failure is recorded. Where different plausible product units, intended uses, causal pathways, outcome claims or counterfactuals could change the result, each is tested separately. The engine may not select only the favourable formulation.

## 1. The whole test

**A product qualifies when its normal use makes a material contribution to an outcome important enough to matter, or creates a material new technical capability needed to deliver such outcomes at scale; the contribution is additional, real and attributable; it depends on a repeatable technical capability built by the company rather than generic deployment or substitutable labour; and the company does not do better when the claimed outcome is worse.**

Directness is not usefulness.

The product does not have to perform or control the final act. A professional, operator, institution or another product may complete the pathway. What matters is whether removing the product makes the qualifying outcome or essential capability materially worse against the relevant counterfactual.

A physical act receives no automatic credit. Information, detection, prediction, selection, guidance and enabling technology receive no automatic penalty. Each is judged by the material work it performs in the pathway.

There are five structural rules. Any one failure produces Excluded. If all five pass, readiness determines Listed or Pending.

## 2. The unit of judgement

The engine never judges a company in the abstract. It judges one defined unit:

**company × product or process × material version × intended use × claimed qualifying outcome × economic model**

A different product, materially different version, intended use, outcome claim or economic model receives a separate judgement.

A research direction, mission, platform description or unspecified future product is not a unit of judgement. An identifiable existing product or process may be judged before commercialisation where its intended use, technical mechanism, outcome claim and verification route are sufficiently defined.

Every material revenue stream connected to the judged product and problem is included. An aligned minor stream cannot be selected while a materially misaligned stream is omitted.

A company-level status is a display aggregation, never a substitute for product-level judgement:

- **Listed** where at least one material current unit is Listed;
- **Pending** where no material current unit is Listed and at least one is Pending;
- **Excluded** only where every identified material current unit is Excluded and none remains unresolved;
- no company-level verdict in every other case.

The public entry states which unit produces the displayed status. A verdict for one unit never extends automatically to another.

**Normal use** means use by the intended operator, for the intended beneficiary, under the conditions in which the product is ordinarily supplied or intended to be supplied. It excludes exceptional supervision, unrepeatable effort, behaviour introduced solely to demonstrate success and work that is neither required nor supported by the product’s operating model.

## 3. What is important enough

A product can pass the importance threshold through either of two routes. It must pass at least one.

### 3.1 Essential-outcome route

The product addresses a problem that materially:

- ends life or shortens expected life;
- reduces health, healthy or independent life, development, or a material physical, cognitive or functional capacity;
- increases the severity, duration, recurrence or progression of such a loss;
- reduces a living system’s ability to persist, regenerate or support life;
- depletes or degrades a physical stock, state or process on which living beings depend faster than it is restored;
- increases the probability, scale or irreversibility of one of those conditions.

A qualifying outcome prevents, reduces, delays, restores, preserves, substitutes or sustains one of those conditions.

Cure is not required. Prevention, continuing support, substitution and preservation qualify where they materially change life, health, capacity, independence, persistence, regeneration or the ability to support life.

Comfort, convenience, output and administrative efficiency alone do not qualify.

### 3.2 Frontier-capability route

Where the connection to a living outcome is diffuse or indirect, a product may instead qualify by materially changing a binding technical limit in an essential physical capability whose insufficiency predictably causes one or more essential-outcome deficits.

The change must cross a meaningful feasibility, performance, accessibility, reliability, safety, resource or scale constraint. It must make an important capability possible, materially better or materially more accessible in a way that the best proven relevant alternative does not.

Ordinary optimisation of utilisation, scheduling, consumption, operating cost or process efficiency does not pass this route. A cumulative improvement does not become a frontier advance merely because it can be multiplied by hypothetical universal adoption.

### 3.3 Primary or inseparable benefit

The qualifying outcome or frontier capability must be a principal intended function of the product or an inseparable consequence of normal use.

A remote, incidental, optional or second-order externality does not qualify. The product may not enter through a minor beneficial side effect while its ordinary value is realised elsewhere.

A derived emissions reduction or customer-side efficiency measure does not cross the importance threshold by itself. It must evidence a material essential outcome or frontier capability under the rules as written.

### 3.4 Importance order

Importance is a threshold, not a numerical score.

The order for close judgements is:

1. severity;
2. irreversibility;
3. material improvement per affected unit;
4. realistic reach;
5. durability.

Severity outweighs frequency. At equal severity, preventing an irreversible loss outweighs delaying a reversible one. A material contribution to a severe problem may qualify at limited reach. A contribution to a less severe, reversible, diffuse or indirect problem requires substantially greater additionality, reach and durability.

No arithmetic score is computed. The entry must state why the threshold is or is not crossed.

### 3.5 Realistic scale

Materiality is judged at the scale supported by the product’s demonstrated or credibly reachable production, economics, deployment model and resource requirements.

A trivial unit effect multiplied by unsupported global adoption does not qualify. A severe unit-level outcome does not need global scale to matter.

## 4. Counterfactuals, references and value

### 4.1 Outcome counterfactual

Every outcome claim is compared with what would otherwise happen to the same affected entity under ordinary conditions.

The outcome counterfactual is the most credible accessible alternative. No action is used only where no relevant alternative is ordinarily accessible. An idealised alternative may not be used to make the product look worse, and an artificially weak alternative may not be used to make it look better.

### 4.2 Technical frontier reference

Every frontier or technical-additionality claim is also compared with the best proven relevant capability, not merely with the average product, current customer practice or absence of deployment.

A product does not create a frontier advance by distributing, financing, installing or operating a capability already available from another supplier.

### 4.3 Materiality threshold

A change is material only where it is consequential to the affected living being, defined population, living system or essential physical capability. It must exceed ordinary variation, measurement noise and a merely detectable or commercially convenient difference.

The materiality threshold is stated before the evidence is judged. Its basis must be external to the company’s own performance claim.

### 4.4 Value

Value is the qualifying outcome or essential capability delivered after transferred harms are included, relative to the resources and burdens required to deliver it.

Value is not customer savings, revenue, adoption, reported activity, a derived impact quantity or a cross-company score.

A product may qualify by preserving a qualifying outcome while materially reducing the resources or burden required to deliver it only where:

1. non-inferiority against the relevant outcome counterfactual is established;
2. the reduction is caused by the company’s own technical capability;
3. the reduction materially increases access, delivery, continuity or durability, or reduces a burden borne by the affected living being or living system;
4. the claimed effect is realised rather than merely available to the payer;
5. the advantage remains without a temporary transfer, accounting convention or policy-made value.

Savings retained entirely by another party do not create a qualifying outcome.

### 4.5 Transferred harm

All material, direct and reasonably foreseeable effects of normal use enter the judgement.

A deficit moved to another living being, living system, place or time is not removed. The product cannot qualify by selecting one favourable measure while omitting an equal or greater qualifying harm caused by the same use.
