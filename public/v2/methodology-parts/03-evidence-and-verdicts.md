## 6. Evidence follows the product’s causal position

The engine does not impose one evidence form on every product. It measures the company’s own material contribution and requires an independently established bridge to the qualifying outcome.

Evidence burden increases with causal distance, uncertainty and plausible confounding. It does not increase merely because the contribution is informational rather than physical.

### 6.1 Direct contribution

Where the product directly changes the qualifying biological, functional, ecological or physical state, evidence must measure that change or a valid intermediate outcome against the relevant counterfactual.

### 6.2 Decision or information contribution

Where the product changes a consequential decision or response, Listed evidence must establish:

1. a product-specific material change in the quality, probability, timing or accessibility of that decision or response under intended use;
2. actual use or externally controlled verification in the defined pathway;
3. an independent causal bridge from the changed step to the qualifying outcome.

The company does not need to re-prove an entire downstream causal relationship already independently established for the relevant context.

Technical accuracy in isolation is insufficient where it does not establish a material change in the consequential step.

### 6.3 Enabling contribution

Where the product changes a necessary upstream technical step, Listed evidence must establish:

1. a material product-specific advantage against the technical frontier reference;
2. external use or verification of that advantage in the defined pathway;
3. observed conversion into consequential downstream work;
4. an independent bridge from that work to the qualifying outcome;
5. an evidence burden proportionate to the remaining contingent steps.

### 6.4 Frontier-capability contribution

Where the product qualifies through the frontier-capability route, Listed evidence must establish:

1. the binding limit;
2. the best proven relevant reference;
3. the magnitude and durability of the advance;
4. performance under conditions representative of intended use;
5. a credible path to production and deployment at the scale on which materiality is claimed;
6. why the advance changes an essential physical capability rather than merely optimising an existing system.

### 6.5 Independence

Independence concerns control, not payment alone.

A process may be funded by the company where the company does not solely control the protocol, conduct, data access, analysis and reporting, and material conflicts are disclosed.

Company-controlled evidence may establish what the product is, how it is intended to work, whether it is available and how the company earns. It cannot by itself establish the independent material contribution required for Listed.

### 6.6 Attribution

The evidence must identify:

- the affected entity or essential capability;
- the product version and intended use;
- the materiality threshold;
- the outcome counterfactual and technical reference;
- the observed direction and magnitude of change;
- the basis for attributing that change to the product;
- uncertainty, confounding and transferred harm;
- the causal bridge where the product does not directly produce the final outcome.

No single study design is mandatory in every case. The method must be capable of identifying the product’s material contribution.

Commercial adoption, revenue, engagement, customer satisfaction, regulatory permission, publication, technical performance and company-reported savings do not establish a qualifying contribution by themselves.

### 6.7 Conflicting and current evidence

Evidence must apply to the current product-use unit. A material change in version, use, operator, pathway, outcome claim or economic model requires a new judgement or a written, publicly auditable transfer argument.

Material contradictory evidence is never omitted. The signed reasoning explains why it changes, does not change or prevents the verdict.

A product cannot be Listed while stronger relevant evidence supports no material contribution or net harm.

## 7. Verdicts

There are exactly three public verdicts: **Listed, Pending and Excluded**. There is no composite score.

### 7.1 Listed

A product-use unit is Listed only where:

1. R1, R2, R3, R4 and R5 all pass;
2. the product is in ordinary arm’s-length use outside a company-controlled demonstration, or the judged contribution is being delivered recurrently to an external user under the intended operating model;
3. independent evidence measures the product’s material contribution under the applicable evidence route in §6;
4. the causal bridge to the qualifying outcome is independently established where the product does not directly produce the final outcome;
5. the evidence applies to the product version, intended use and claimed advantage being judged, or a written transfer argument establishes why it applies;
6. no stronger material evidence establishes no benefit or net harm.

Ordinary arm’s-length use does not require a particular payment form. An external party must use the product for its intended purpose and bear the consequences of that use under conditions that can recur without exceptional company-controlled effort.

### 7.2 Pending

A product-use unit is Pending only where:

1. all five structural rules pass;
2. an identifiable product or process exists;
3. the intended use, affected entity, qualifying outcome or frontier capability, materiality threshold, counterfactual and technical reference are sufficiently defined to be tested;
4. one or more Listed readiness conditions are missing, including where applicable ordinary use, product-specific contribution evidence, causal-bridge validation, external conversion, frontier validation or credible scale evidence;
5. every missing condition is stated as an observable threshold;
6. a publicly checkable verification route and dated review point are recorded.

Pending means **not yet verified or available**, not promising, early, desirable or unclear.

A generic reservation is invalid. The entry states:

- exactly what is missing;
- what result would satisfy it;
- who or what would benefit;
- the required magnitude;
- the product version and intended use;
- the active verification route;
- the next milestone and review date.

A Pending entry may exist before ordinary commercial use. It may not exist for a generic future possibility with no identifiable product, defined contribution or checkable path.

Passing the review date triggers re-judgement. It never creates automatic Excluded status.

### 7.3 Excluded

A product-use unit is Excluded where one or more of R1–R5 fails.

A structural failure means **not like this**. Additional readiness evidence cannot cure it. The verdict can change only where:

- the product, intended use, claimed contribution, technical mechanism or economic model changes materially; or
- new evidence establishes that the factual basis of the failure was wrong.

Strong evidence that the current product falls below the stated materiality or additionality threshold can produce an R1 or R2 failure. Missing evidence alone cannot.

### 7.4 No verdict

No verdict is not a fourth public verdict. It is the absence of a judgement.

It applies where:

- the unit of judgement is not identifiable;
- a material structural fact remains unresolved after the required research passes;
- the available public evidence cannot establish either a Pass or Fail for a structural rule.

No verdict must never be converted into Pending or Excluded merely to complete the database.

Every rule result is recorded as Pass, Fail or Unresolved. Listed and Pending require five Pass results. Excluded requires at least one Fail. An Unresolved result can never be displayed as Pass.
