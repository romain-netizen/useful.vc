## 11. Write-time invariants

The database, not the prompt alone, enforces the following:

1. Listed can be written only with five Pass results and every Listed readiness condition satisfied.
2. Pending can be written only with five Pass results, at least one named readiness gap, an observable threshold and verification route for every gap, and a review date.
3. Excluded can be written only with at least one named R1–R5 failure and the mechanism producing it.
4. Missing evidence, missing commercialisation, regulatory timing, company stage or company status can never write Excluded by themselves.
5. Structural uncertainty can never write Pending.
6. Direct physical action can never substitute for an R1 importance pass or an R2 material-contribution pass.
7. Informational or enabling causal position can never create an automatic failure.
8. A derived impact quantity, customer saving or policy-made value can never satisfy R1 or R3 by itself.
9. A routing attribute can never alter a verdict.
10. A company-level publication decision can never overwrite a product-level verdict.
11. A methodology or prompt hash mismatch blocks every new verdict write.
12. Every change creates a new append-only record. Earlier records are never silently overwritten.
13. The public entry reads only the latest signed verdict under the active methodology version.
14. Legacy criteria and verdicts remain historical records and cannot satisfy a current publication rule.

The deterministic order is:

1. identify the unit of judgement;
2. define the affected entity, qualifying problem and admission route;
3. set the importance and materiality thresholds;
4. define the outcome counterfactual and technical frontier reference;
5. apply R1;
6. map the product’s contribution and apply R2;
7. identify the real endpoint and apply R3;
8. apply the technical removal and scaling tests under R4;
9. apply the economic-alignment test under R5;
10. if any rule fails, write Excluded;
11. if no rule fails and any rule remains Unresolved, write no verdict;
12. if all five pass, apply the readiness and evidence route;
13. write Listed only when every Listed condition holds;
14. write Pending only when every Pending condition holds;
15. apply attributes and publication facts after the verdict;
16. require a named human signature before publication.

No later step may alter an earlier rule result.

## 12. What the engine does not judge

The engine does not judge a product by:

- its sector or business category;
- whether its immediate output is physical or informational;
- the identity, prestige or type of its customer, payer, operator or beneficiary;
- the contract or payment form;
- the founders, team, politics, board or declared values;
- political attention, public popularity or moral signalling;
- ownership, solvency, fundraising, maturity label, corporate activity or publication status;
- the identity of its investors or whether useful.vc has money in it;
- intention, mission, disclosure quality or likelihood of commercial success.

These facts may be published separately where relevant. They cannot replace the written tests.

There are no case-specific exceptions, rescue clauses or discretionary overrides.

No expected distribution of Listed, Pending or Excluded products is written in advance. No target count, pass rate or portfolio preference may influence an individual verdict.

## 13. Settled factual positions

A recurring factual premise may be maintained in a separate public, cited and versioned evidence register.

A settled position may supply a premise used inside a rule. It cannot:

- decide a company verdict;
- create a category-level pass or failure;
- remove a rule;
- lower an evidence burden for a favoured product;
- bypass product-specific application of R1–R5.

Every settled position states its scope, evidence, date, named owner and review trigger. It remains challengeable by evidence.

A material change to a settled position triggers re-judgement of every entry that relied on it.

## 14. Blind doctrine validation

No methodology version becomes active solely because its wording appears coherent.

Before activation it must pass a held-out regression suite that is not included in the runtime classifier prompt and is not visible to the model applying live verdicts.

The suite contains matched product-use records designed to test:

- invariance to company name, sector label, buyer identity, contract form and useful.vc relationship;
- invariance to whether the immediate act is physical or informational after importance and material contribution are held constant;
- sensitivity to changes in problem severity, irreversibility, realistic reach and durability;
- sensitivity to changes in additionality against the real counterfactual and technical frontier;
- sensitivity to whether the endpoint is real or merely financial, legal, accounting or representational;
- sensitivity to whether the company builds technical leverage or merely deploys available capability;
- sensitivity to economic misalignment;
- correct separation of structural failure, readiness gap and unresolved research.

Matched pairs must differ in only one material fact wherever possible. The expected change in verdict must follow from that fact alone.

The regression suite tests the doctrine and the prompt separately. A passing doctrine with a failing prompt is not activated. A passing prompt with a defective doctrine is not activated.

Any systematic preference for causal proximity over material contribution, or for a directly measured proxy over a more important evidenced outcome, blocks activation.

Regression failures are corrected by changing the general rule or implementation for every case. They are never repaired by inserting named exceptions.

## 15. Challenging, correcting and re-judging

The full reasoning and supporting public evidence behind every published entry are public.

A challenge can change an entry by showing that:

- a material fact is wrong;
- stronger evidence changes the factual balance;
- the wrong product-use unit, counterfactual or technical reference was selected;
- a written rule was applied incorrectly.

Intention, reputation, pressure and relationship do not change a verdict.

A correction to evidence changes the affected entry. A change to doctrine is different: it requires a new public methodology version, a new content hash, a new regression run and re-judgement of every affected entry.

No earlier verdict carries precedent or protection.

Re-judgement proceeds in this order:

1. known inconsistencies and regression failures;
2. every currently published entry;
3. entries with materially changed products, uses, evidence or economic models;
4. exclusions whose stated reason is not valid under the current methodology;
5. the remainder.

Where materially equivalent product-use units hold opposite verdicts, both are re-tested. The current written rules decide them, and the unsupported decision moves.

Expected outcomes are never written in advance. A projected count becomes a target, and a target corrupts the engine.
