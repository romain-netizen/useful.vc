Public methodology

How useful.vc decides

We judge products, not slogans, sectors or companies in the abstract. There is no hidden score and no compensation between rules.

A product qualifies when it makes a material contribution to an outcome important enough to matter, or creates a material new technical capability needed to deliver such outcomes at scale.

What exactly is judged?

Each decision concerns one defined unit:

Company × product or process × material version × intended use × claimed outcome × economic model.

A company can therefore have several entries. A useful product does not confer its verdict on the company’s services, deployment work or other products.

Rule 1

Is the outcome important enough?

The product must materially affect life, health, development, independence, an important capacity, the persistence of a living system, or a physical stock on which life depends.

It can also qualify by breaking a binding technical limit in an essential physical capability. That must be a real step change relative to the best proven relevant alternative—not ordinary optimisation, scheduling, incremental efficiency or a minor saving multiplied by imaginary global adoption.

For close judgements we consider severity, irreversibility, improvement per affected entity, realistic reach and durability. Severity comes first.

Carbon reduction is evidence, not an outcome by itself. The company must identify the underlying additional physical or biological change and show that it matters at realistic scale.

Rule 2

Does the product make a material additional contribution?

We remove the product from the ordinary pathway and ask what becomes materially worse: the probability, quality, timing, accessibility or durability of the important outcome; the reliability of a consequential decision; or the performance of an essential capability.

The product does not have to perform or control the final act. A clinician, scientist, operator or another product may complete the pathway.

Diagnostics, decision tools and enabling technologies can qualify when they materially change a defined consequential step and a defensible causal bridge connects that step to the important outcome. A generic report, score, alert or platform with no defined conversion into useful work does not qualify.

Causal distance raises the evidence burden. It does not create automatic exclusion.

Rule 3

Is the claimed value real and intrinsic?

The product’s immediate output may be information, but the claimed value must end through a defined and testable pathway in a biological, functional, ecological or physical result, or in a material frontier advance in an essential capability.

A change only in money, financing, ownership, risk allocation, legal entitlement, certification, accounting, reporting, compliance, exchange or recorded impact does not qualify.

Measurement can support the pathway. It cannot be its endpoint. A policy may encourage real work, but it cannot manufacture usefulness where nothing physical or biological remains different.

Rule 4

Did the company build the technical leverage?

The contribution must depend on a repeatable company-specific product, process or operated technical system.

Remove the company’s own technology. If essentially the same contribution and advantage can be obtained at comparable quality, reliability, speed, scale and resource use from existing products, ordinary integration and interchangeable labour, the company is deploying rather than building.

People may install, supervise, interpret, maintain or use the product. Human involvement is not a failure. The question is whether the company-built technology materially changes what they can reliably achieve.

Financing, leasing, distribution, installation and operation of existing technology do not become technical invention merely because they are performed well.

Rule 5

Does the company win when the outcome improves?

Holding the affected person or living system and its starting condition constant, the company must not earn more because the outcome is worse, later, shorter-lived, repeatedly fails or is transferred elsewhere.

A company may earn more by helping more affected people. Recurring revenue can align where continued use continues to preserve, restore, substitute or sustain the benefit.

The payer and contract form are irrelevant. Outcome-based payment may support the analysis but never proves alignment.

Evidence follows the product’s contribution

We do not force every product through the same evidence format.

  • Direct products: measure the biological, functional, ecological or physical result.
  • Diagnostic and decision products: measure the product-specific change in a consequential decision or response and establish the bridge to the important outcome.
  • Enabling products: establish an advantage against the best relevant alternative, external use, conversion into downstream work and the bridge to the important outcome.
  • Frontier technologies: establish the binding limit, reference capability, magnitude and durability of the advance, realistic operating performance and a credible route to the scale claimed.

Revenue, adoption, regulatory permission, publication, customer satisfaction and technical accuracy may support a file. None proves the important outcome by itself. Material contradictory evidence is shown, not hidden.

The verdicts

Main

All five structural rules pass, the product is used externally under its intended operating model, and independent evidence establishes its material contribution and causal bridge.

Pending

All five structural rules pass, but one or more readiness conditions remain open. The entry must name the exact missing evidence, threshold, verification route, next milestone and review date.

Excluded

At least one structural rule fails. Every failed rule and its reasoning are published. Missing evidence alone cannot create Excluded.

No verdict

Where a material structural fact remains unresolved, no verdict is written. We do not convert uncertainty into Pending or Excluded to complete the database.

Collections are separate from verdicts

The principal public collections are Technology and Biotech & invasive medicine. Drugs, biologics, implants, invasive devices and surgical systems are physically separated from software, robotics, non-invasive health, industrial technology, energy, materials and nature technology.

Collection, country, investor, corporate status and attributes such as Asset or Invasive never determine whether a product qualifies.

Transparency and collaboration

Every public entry identifies the product, intended use, important outcome, counterfactual, technical reference, result under all five rules, readiness gaps, evidence, contradictory evidence, methodology version and named signer.

Anyone can submit a company, add evidence, correct a fact, identify a missing product unit, challenge a counterfactual or dispute the application of a rule.

A company changes when evidence or reasoning changes. A methodology change is different: it requires a new public version, a new hash, held-out consistency tests and re-judgement of every affected product. Named exceptions are prohibited.
