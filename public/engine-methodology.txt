useful.vc — engine methodology
Replaces every earlier selectivity formulation. Same text into the classifier
prompt and the coverage automation prompt, identically.
This document contains no examples, no sector lists and no named cases, and none
may be added to it. Every rule below generates its own instances. A rule that
arrives with a list gets applied to the list and stops being a rule: the engine
starts matching against members instead of testing against the criterion, and
anything outside the list escapes judgement. Where a case seems to need an example
in order to be decided, the rule is badly written. Say so. Do not invent the
example.
The public rules page is the reference text. Where this document and that page
differ, the page wins and this document is corrected.
Nothing here is negotiable case by case. See §8.
1. The question
Does the product do the thing, or does it help someone else maybe do it?
2. The four exclusion rules
Any one fires and the company is Excluded. No averaging, no compensation between
rules, no strength elsewhere.
R1 — The product does the work itself. If the situation only improves after a
third party decides, develops, finances or deploys something further, the company
has sold a possibility. Excluded.
R2 — Whoever pays is not the one helped. If the customer is also the
beneficiary, and what they gain is time or money, the company is improving how a
business runs. Both conditions required. Excluded.
R3 — The company acts on the problem, not on its consequences. Moving money
after harm has occurred, lending against a shortage, or taking a fee on an exchange
leaves the situation as it was. Sustaining someone against a condition that cannot
be cured is not this: it acts on the condition.
R4 — The company's interest runs with the interest of whoever carries the
problem. Including parties who never pay, and non-human ones. Where the company
does better as the affected party does worse, the stated intention changes nothing.
Excluded.
3. What counts as a problem
Three kinds qualify. Nothing else does.
What ends a life or shortens one.
What permanently removes a capacity a living being has, or would have had.
What is consumed faster than it is replaced, among the things living beings
depend on.
Two kinds are excluded without discussion. A condition that exists only inside a
measurement or reporting framework, and anything that improves comfort,
convenience or output.
Ordering among qualifying problems
Severity × number × irreversibility.
A precedence order for close calls, not a quantity to compute. Severity outweighs
frequency. What cannot be undone outweighs what can, at equal size — and this holds
even where the reversible harm is the one currently receiving attention.
Dependence on a rule
If the benefit disappears when a regulation or a regulation-made market
changes, and nothing physical is different afterwards, it was not a physical
benefit.
Physical work that happens to be encouraged by a rule is unaffected. The test is
whether anything remains standing when the rule is withdrawn.
4. Main or Pending
Main — someone outside the company has measured the outcome.
Pending — the four rules hold and no independent measurement exists yet. State
which measurement is missing.
Readiness decides this and only this. Absence of proof is never an exclusion, never
a structural failure, and never enters a criterion. Enforced at write time, not by
instruction.
5. The single suspension
Where the problem ends lives, R1 is suspended. An upstream company may enter at
Pending when the harm it targets is fatal and the work aimed at the remedy is the
step that has been abandoned.
Nothing else is ever suspended. R2, R3 and R4 hold in all circumstances, including
here.
6. Two kinds of exclusion — always state which
R1 says "not yet." Reversed by a result, never by an argument. The threshold:
something originating from the company enters a verification process the company
does not control. A company excluded under R1 is owed that threshold in writing.
R2, R3 and R4 say "not like this." No evidence reverses them. Only a change in
how the company earns does, and that is a different company. A company excluded
under these is owed the truth about its model rather than a milestone it can never
reach.
7. What remains when payment stops — a modifier, never a fifth rule
What is still there when the invoice stops?
Four positions, strongest first: something built that exists without the company;
something physical the company built and operates; something that works while the
licence is paid; someone's time.
The contract form is irrelevant. What remains is what counts, and the question is
answered by what happens on the ground, not by how the company is billed.
Within the third position, one discriminator: was something invented that nobody
knew how to do, or were known parts assembled well. Evidence for the first is
external and verifiable.
This never excludes. Used as a rule it would remove work delivered by people, which
can pass all four rules. It does two things: it settles ties between otherwise
equivalent files, and it raises the bar for Main, since work delivered by people
must additionally show the benefit persists once the engagement ends.
8. No exceptions
The rules are written. Borderline cases are settled with the rules as they stand.
Disagreement about a case does not reopen the doctrine. Do not add carve-outs, do
not create new statuses, do not introduce a category as a reason.
Prohibited as a stated reason: anything that names a sector, a business
category, a customer type or a stage of maturity as the ground of the verdict. Each
substitutes a judgement about a class for a judgement about a company.
Prohibited: any verdict derived from who the customer is. The identity of the
buyer confers nothing and condemns nothing.
Prohibited: any fact about the company's own situation entering a verdict about
its product. Solvency, ownership, activity and publication status are held apart
and cannot reach a criterion.
9. Every verdict records
Which rule fired, and whether it is "not yet" or "not like this". For R1, the
threshold that would reverse it. For the others, what in the model produces the
failure. What we consider the equivalent alternative, and the dimension on which we
see no advantage. Who bears the deficit, and whether they can escape it. For
Pending, what a third party would have to measure.
A verdict naming no rule is not a verdict. A company kept without a written reason
is the same defect as a company excluded without one, and harder to notice.
Field names belong to the schema, not to this document.
10. Re-judgement
Every company is judged by this text, including every company already decided. A
decision taken under an earlier formulation carries no precedent and grants no
protection.
Order of work: the cases known to be inconsistent, then everything published, then
the remainder. Where two companies with the same model hold opposite verdicts, the
rules decide both, and the older decision is the one that moves.
Expected outcomes are not written here. A projected count becomes a target, and a
target is met.
